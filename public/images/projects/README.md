# Project photos

Real job photos go here, one folder-flat set per project, named after the case
study slug — for example `moore-lot-01.jpg`, `moore-lot-02.jpg`.

Every image on the site today is stock. Replacing it with real work is the
highest-value content task outstanding, because it feeds three places at once:
these case study pages, the Google Business Profile, and the homepage.

## What to shoot

For each completed project, five to eight photos covering:

1. **Wide establishing shot** of the finished work, showing scale.
2. **Work in progress** with crew and equipment visible — this is the shot that
   proves the work is yours and not stock.
3. **A detail** that shows quality: a formed edge, joint, tie-in, or finish.
4. **Branded equipment or trucks** on site.
5. **The finished surface or structure** from the angle a user would see it.

## Requirements

- **Format:** JPG for photos.
- **Minimum width:** 1600px. Larger is fine; Next resizes automatically.
- **Orientation:** landscape for heroes and cards, since layouts use 4:3 and 16:9.
- **No stock photography**, and no images you do not hold the rights to.
- **Get client permission** before publishing anything identifying a school,
  municipality, or tenant.

## Alt text

Write the `alt` in the case study entry to describe what is visible, not to
repeat keywords. "Crew placing asphalt across a school parking lot" is useful to
both a screen reader and image search; "asphalt paving Oklahoma" is not.
